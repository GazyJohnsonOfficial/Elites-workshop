These Terms of Enrolment apply when you register for the Data Analysis Programme run by Elites Tech Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). Other services, such as consulting, staffing and corporate training, are agreed separately in writing with each client, and nothing on this website is an offer or a price. By submitting the registration form you confirm that you have read and agree to them.

2. The programme

  • Start date: November 10, 2026.
  • Duration: 3 months (12 weeks).
  • Schedule: 3 live sessions per week, 1 hour each (36 sessions in total). Session days and times are confirmed by email before the start date.
  • Format: online, live on Zoom.
  • Content: databases and SQL; Python, Excel and VBA; ETL with Alteryx; Tableau and Power BI; and a capstone project.
  • Tuition: see the Tuition and Fees page.

We may make reasonable changes to content, schedule, tools or instructors, and we will tell you by email.

3. Registration and your place

Submitting the registration form records your interest and reserves your place for consideration. Your place is confirmed when our team confirms it to you in writing (email is sufficient) and payment arrangements are agreed. We may decline a registration or place you on a waiting list, for example if the programme is full.

After registering you receive a reference number. Please quote it when you contact us.

4. Tuition and payment

Tuition is USD 1,000 for the full programme (or two instalments of USD 500), as shown on the Tuition and Fees page. Payments can be completed securely online via credit/debit card, Mobile Money, or bank transfer through our Online Checkout, or arranged directly with our admissions team. Every payment is issued an official reference number and receipt.

5. Changes, withdrawals and refunds

You can ask to change or cancel your registration at any time by contacting us. Conditions for withdrawal, deferral and refunds are confirmed by our team when your place is confirmed, and form part of your enrolment.

If we need to cancel or reschedule the programme, we will tell you as soon as possible and discuss your options with you.
