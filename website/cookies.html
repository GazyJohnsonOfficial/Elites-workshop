1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. Websites can also use similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage. This policy explains which of these our website uses and why.

2. What we store on your device

NameTypePurposeHow longCategory
ets_consent_v1Local storageRemembers your cookie choice (whether you allowed analytics, and when you chose).12 months, then we ask againEssential
ets_last_reg, ets_last_contactSession storageA timestamp that stops the same form being sent repeatedly by mistake or by bots.Until you close the tabEssential
ets_reg_emailsSession storageRemembers the email address you just registered with, to prevent an accidental duplicate registration.Until you close the tabEssential

Essential items are needed for the website and its forms to work safely, so they do not require consent. We do not set any traditional cookies.

If you submit a form, your browser sends the details you entered to our email delivery provider, EmailJS. This happens only when you submit a form. See the Privacy Policy.

3. What we do not use

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics today. If we add analytics in the future, it will stay off until you allow it in the cookie preferences, and this policy will be updated.
  • No fonts, scripts or images loaded from third-party servers. They are all served from this website.

Links to WhatsApp and social media open those services in a new tab. Their own cookie policies apply there.
