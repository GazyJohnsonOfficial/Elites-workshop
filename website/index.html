How We Deliver Projects in Four Steps

Every consulting, migration, and staffing engagement follows a clear four-stage process.

  1. Initial Technical Review

    We inspect your existing servers, databases, security policies, or staffing requirements and identify immediate risks and cost savings.

  2. Written Scope & Architecture

    We provide a written technical proposal with architecture diagrams, fixed milestones, timeline estimates, and transparent pricing.

  3. Implementation & Testing

    Our engineers build, migrate, secure, or staff your environment in staged releases with rollback plans to prevent downtime.

  4. Handover & 24/7 Support

    We train your internal team, deliver full system documentation, and provide ongoing 24/7 monitoring and backup management.
