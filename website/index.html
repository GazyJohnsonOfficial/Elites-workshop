How We Work

A Clear Four-Step Delivery Process

Every consulting, migration, and staffing engagement follows four structured phases with defined deliverables and transparent pricing.

  1. Technical Audit & Scope

    We inspect your existing servers, databases, security settings, and monthly cloud spend to identify immediate risks and cost-saving opportunities.

  2. Architecture & Fixed Roadmap

    Our architects deliver a written system blueprint, migration schedule, security checklist, and projected monthly hosting cost before implementation begins.

  3. Build, Migrate & Test

    Our engineers provision the cloud environment, automate deployments with Terraform, migrate your data, and run load and security tests prior to cutover.

  4. 24/7 Support & Team Handover

    We provide ongoing uptime monitoring, automated backups, and live walkthroughs so your internal staff can operate the system with confidence.
